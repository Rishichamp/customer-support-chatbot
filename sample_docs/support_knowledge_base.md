# Customer Support Knowledge Base

## Order and Shipping FAQs

### How long does delivery take?

**Standard Shipping (Free on orders above ₹500)**
- Metro cities: 3-5 business days
- Other cities: 5-7 business days
- Remote areas: 7-10 business days

**Express Shipping (₹149 flat)**
- Metro cities: 1-2 business days
- Available in Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad

**Same-Day Delivery (₹249 flat)**
- Available in select metro areas
- Orders must be placed before 12:00 PM IST

### My order hasn't arrived. What should I do?

1. Check your tracking number in the confirmation email
2. Visit track.company.com or our app to see real-time status
3. If tracking shows "Out for Delivery" — your order will arrive today
4. If tracking shows "Attempted Delivery" — rescheduling options are available via the app
5. If no tracking update for 3+ days after dispatch — contact support with your order ID

### Can I change my delivery address after placing an order?

Address changes are only possible **before the order is dispatched**. Once dispatched, the address cannot be changed. Contact support within 2 hours of placing your order to request an address change.

### What happens if my package is lost in transit?

If your package shows no movement for 7+ business days:
1. File a "Lost Shipment" claim in your account under Orders → Report Issue
2. We will investigate with the courier within 3 business days
3. If confirmed lost, we will either resend the item or issue a full refund within 5 business days

## Returns and Refunds

### What is the return policy?

You may return most items within **30 days** of delivery for a full refund.

**Eligible for return:**
- Products in original, unused condition with all packaging
- Defective or damaged items (any condition)
- Items not matching product description

**NOT eligible for return:**
- Digital products (software licenses, ebook access, online courses)
- Perishables (food, plants)
- Customised or personalised items
- Intimate or sanitary goods

### How do I initiate a return?

1. Log in to your account at account.company.com
2. Go to Orders → select the order → click "Request Return"
3. Select the reason for return
4. Print the prepaid return shipping label (emailed to you)
5. Drop the package at any authorised courier location

Returns are processed within 5-7 business days of receiving the item.

### When will I receive my refund?

After your return is received and inspected:
- **Credit/Debit card**: 3-5 business days
- **Store credit**: Issued immediately on approval
- **Bank transfer (NEFT)**: 7-10 business days
- **UPI**: 1-3 business days

### Can I exchange instead of returning?

Yes. During the return process, select "Exchange" instead of "Refund". Exchanges are available for:
- Size/variant issues
- Defective items
- Wrong item received

## Billing and Payment Issues

### My payment failed but amount was deducted. What do I do?

This is a temporary hold (not a charge). The amount will automatically be reversed within:
- Credit/Debit card: 3-7 business days
- UPI: 1-3 business days
- Net banking: 3-5 business days

If not reversed after the above period, contact your bank with the transaction reference number.

### How do I get a GST invoice?

1. Add your GSTIN to your Business Profile (account.company.com → Business Settings)
2. GST invoices are auto-generated and available under Orders → Download Invoice
3. For past orders without GSTIN: submit a request at billing@company.com with order ID and GSTIN

### What payment methods are accepted?

- Credit cards: Visa, Mastercard, Amex, RuPay
- Debit cards: All major Indian banks
- UPI: PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, BHIM
- Net banking: 50+ banks
- EMI: Available on credit cards (3, 6, 9, 12 months) for orders above ₹3,000
- Buy Now Pay Later: LazyPay, ZestMoney, Simpl

## Account and Technical Support

### How do I reset my password?

1. Go to login.company.com
2. Click "Forgot Password"
3. Enter your registered email
4. Click the reset link in the email (valid for 24 hours)
5. Set a new password

If you don't receive the email within 5 minutes, check your spam folder or contact support.

### How do I update my mobile number?

Mobile number changes require OTP verification on both old and new numbers.
1. Go to Account Settings → Contact Information
2. Click "Change Mobile Number"
3. Verify old number with OTP, then verify new number
4. Change takes effect immediately

### I can't log in to my account. What should I do?

Common causes and solutions:
- **Wrong password**: Use "Forgot Password" to reset
- **Account locked**: After 5 failed attempts, account is locked for 30 minutes
- **Email not found**: You may have registered with a different email address
- **2FA issues**: If you lost access to your authenticator app, contact support with government ID proof

### The app/website is not loading. What should I do?

1. Clear browser cache and cookies
2. Try a different browser or incognito mode
3. Check your internet connection
4. Disable VPN if active
5. Try the mobile app if website is down
6. Check status.company.com for any ongoing outages
